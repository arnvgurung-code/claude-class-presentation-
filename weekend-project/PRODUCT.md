# Product

The Weekend Project: a series of curated evening parties ("chapters"), each with its own theme word, intentionally built DJ sets, and the venue revealed close to the night.

## Platform
Instagram posts (1080×1350) and stories (1080×1920); occasionally printed.

## Users
Young adults in the organisers' city who want a curated night out with people new to each other; many first-timers ("newbies'" was chapter one). They scroll Instagram on phones, at night, and decide in a glance.

## Product Purpose
Sell tickets in stages (save the date, early bird for limited spots, general tickets, countdown) and make each chapter feel like an event worth not missing.

## Positioning
Not a club night and not a corporate Christmas do: a small, designed, slightly secretive evening with a playful voice.

## Operating Context
Chapter two is the Christmas edition, theme word "elves'". Early bird tickets are out, spots are limited, venue and time are to be announced. Month assumed December 2026; no price or closing date given.

## Capabilities and Constraints
No event photographs supplied for chapter two. Image generation is unavailable (the supplied key was rejected), so art is authored in code.

## Brand Commitments
Name set as "THE WEEKEND PROJECT", stacked, with "by". Each chapter carries "chapter N" plus a lowercase theme word with an apostrophe. Voice: lowercase, short, playful, never salesy. The user asked that chapter two look new and creative, not a copy of chapter one.

## Evidence on Hand
Eight chapter-one posters: geometric bold sans, arched lockups, grey halftone collage, stippled orange stars, blue asterisks, duotone party photos.

## Product Principles
Each chapter gets its own world; the name and voice carry the series. Facts stay true: nothing invented about price, venue or time.
