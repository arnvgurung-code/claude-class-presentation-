## Direction contract

THESIS: the series continues, not a generic Christmas flyer. It refuses red-and-green clip art, snowflakes and script fonts; Christmas arrives through objects printed in the brand's halftone (an elf hat, a disco-ball bauble), not through a new palette.

OWN-WORLD: grey halftone "leopard" ground, one translucent grey panel, black-ink halftone cut-outs on white paper with a paper margin, stippled orange starburst, small stippled blue asterisks, salmon numerals, charcoal + off-white bold geometric sans (Montserrat 700/800), lowercase arched lockup, stacked uppercase wordmark.

STORY: recognise the series at a glance (the arch), read "elves'" as the theme, see that early bird is out and spots are limited, know venue and time come later, know who throws it.
