# The Weekend Project — poster system

Each chapter gets its own world; the name, the `chapter N + theme word'` lockup and the voice carry the series. Chapter one: grey halftone collage (below). Chapter two ("elves'"): knitted jumper (next section).

## Chapter two world: knit

- **Chart:** 72 stitches across (15px), rows of ~16.5px; every cell is a V-stitch (two shaded, rotated legs over a dark gap) with yarn fuzz; a soft vignette.
- **Yarn:** body `#2159b6` (brand blue), lettering `#f1e8d6`, stars `#ec7a3f`, hats `#ee8b6f`, bands `#2a292d`, rib `#1b4a99`.
- **Type in the knit:** an 8-row pixel font, lowercase; the theme word at double scale. Uppercase only for the action word (OUT).
- **Bands:** zigzag + checker at the yoke, a row of 8-point stars, a row of elf hats, rib at the hem.
- **Small text:** a sewn-on woven label (`#f3ede0`, dashed orange thread inset, neutral drop shadow, −2° rotation, Montserrat 700/800).

## Chapter one world: halftone collage

Recorded from the "chapter one — newbies'" posters and built into "chapter two — elves'". Formats: Instagram post 1080×1350, story 1080×1920 (keep type out of the story's top 220px and bottom 250px).

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Job |
|---|---|---|
| ground | `#c4c3c1` + halftone "leopard" dots (`#5c5c5e`–`#969696`, white specks) | the page |
| panel | `rgba(214,213,211,.78)` | one translucent block carrying ticket facts |
| ink | `#2a292d` | lockup part one, ticket copy |
| paper | `#fbfaf6` | cut-out paper, lockup part two, wordmark |
| salmon | `#ee8b6f` | numerals (dates) |
| orange | `#ec7a3f` | stippled 8-point starburst only |
| blue | `#1f62c4` | small stippled asterisks, "OUT" |

## Type

Montserrat 800 throughout (stand-in for the Gotham-like face in past posters), tight leading 0.8–0.86, tracking −0.02 to −0.045em.
- **Lockup:** lowercase, set on an arc; "chapter N" in ink, theme word in paper with its apostrophe ("newbies'", "elves'").
- **Wordmark:** "by" in ink, then THE / WEEKEND / PROJECT stacked uppercase in paper, bottom-left.
- **Ticket copy:** stepped lines ("early bird / tickets / OUT"); the action word in blue italic.
- **Dates:** salmon numerals, `DD.MM.YYYY` or `MM.YYYY`, two lines when stacked.

## Material

- Collage objects are black-ink halftone (45° screen, 7–10px cell) on white paper cut-outs with a ~12px paper margin.
- One hero object enters from an edge and bleeds off it.
- Stars are stippled (random dots, dense centre, scattered edges), never solid vectors.
- No red-and-green holiday palette: seasons arrive through the objects, not new colours.
