Actors play customers · No proof of consent · Real calls wiped
AI agents now answer India’s phones, but they learned from scripts and foreign data. Three gaps: actors play customers, real recordings have no proof of consent, and call centres delete millions of real hours.

Call centres get recording, speech analytics, a quality dashboard and headsets for free. After each call, the caller can press 1 to allow AI training. We label those calls and license them to AI companies at ₹2,800 an hour.
