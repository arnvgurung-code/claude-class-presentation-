# Placet

Placet (Latin for "it pleases", the word used to vote yes) builds consented call data for AI.

## What it does

Indian call centres get recording, speech analytics, a quality dashboard and headsets for free
(about ₹9 lakh of software and hardware per centre). After each support call a 15-second message
asks the caller to press 1 to allow AI training. Consent is logged with the call ID and an SMS
receipt; no answer or a 2 deletes the audio within 24 hours. Placet mutes names, labels each call
for intent, emotion, decision and outcome, and licenses the hours to AI companies at ₹2,800 an
hour on average, each hour sold about twice.

## Who the deck is for

Accelerator partners and investors (Antler India, Peak XV Surge, Techstars, Y Combinator,
100X.VC) and a class audience. They must believe three things: the data is real and consented,
buyers already pay for it, and ₹1.41 crore ($150K) once carries year one before sales pay.

## Proof the deck carries

- A live call demo: two tracks, labels filling in, the press-1 question, a consent receipt.
- A month-by-month model, 2027 to 2031, with scenarios the presenter can change live.
- Named deals that show buyers already pay (Google, micro1, Mercor, Neon, Babel Audio).

## Constraints

- Every figure comes from the model in the deck's script or the sources slide; nothing invented.
- Money shows in rupees (lakh and crore) with a one-key switch to dollars at $1 = ₹94.
- Presented on a projector with a clicker: every build advances on one click.
